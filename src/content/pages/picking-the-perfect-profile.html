Edge Guide

Picking the perfect countertop profile

Home / Profile Guide

The edge profile you choose can change the entire look of your countertop. It affects the shape, feel, and style of the surface, and it can make a kitchen feel more modern, more traditional, or more upscale without changing the stone itself.

At T Counters, we often help homeowners compare profiles like eased, bullnose, bevel, and waterfall to find the right finish for their lifestyle and design goals. A profile should feel appropriate for the room, comfortable in daily use, and visually consistent with the rest of the space.

Eased

Eased edges have a subtle angled finish that is clean, modern, and easy to live with. This is one of the most versatile profiles for kitchen countertops and works well in a wide range of home styles.

1/4" Round

A lightly rounded edge keeps the counter soft and comfortable while maintaining a clean, tailored appearance for kitchens and baths.

Half Bullnose

Half bullnose offers a classic, softly rounded finish that feels inviting and traditional without feeling too ornate.

Full Bullnose

Full bullnose creates a smooth, rounded edge that feels warm, classic, and especially comfortable in family homes.

1/2" Bevel

A beveled edge gives a subtle chamfer for a slightly more polished, custom look without becoming overly decorative.

114" Bevel

This beveled profile adds crisp detail and a more custom, architectural finish for premium kitchens and baths.

Bevel Top & Bottom

Bevel top and bottom gives the stone more visible profile detail and a layered, design-focused finish.

Ogee

Ogee profiles bring classic curves and a decorative look to kitchens, baths, and statement islands.

Flat Ogee

Flat ogee keeps the elegance of an ogee profile while creating a slightly cleaner, more understated silhouette.

Ogee Bull

Ogee bull blends a soft rounded silhouette with decorative curved detail, making it a standout choice for statement counters.

Round Top & Bottom

This profile creates a strong, finished edge with a consistent rounded top and bottom, ideal for a refined, custom look.

Dom Bullnose

Dom bullnose adds a more dramatic rounded profile with a polished, substantial finish that stands out beautifully in high-end spaces.

How to choose the right profile

The best profile for your home often comes down to how you use the space and the style you want to achieve. Eased edges are a great all-around choice for everyday kitchens, bullnose feels softer and more classic, and waterfall options create a more dramatic statement. If you want something in between, bevel edges offer a little extra detail without becoming too ornate.

For a clean, modern look

Eased edges or a simple mitered profile often work best when you want the stone to feel streamlined and contemporary.

For a classic or family-friendly style

Bullnose profiles feel softer and more welcoming, making them a popular option in busy households and traditional kitchens.

For a more decorative finish

Bevel edges provide extra detail and definition, especially when the stone is a focal point in the room.

For a high-impact kitchen island

Waterfall and mitered edges create a dramatic statement and make islands feel more architectural and custom.

Helpful note

The best edge is the one that fits your space and your daily life

The profile should complement the material, the room, and how you use the counter. We can help you compare options side by side so you can choose something that feels just right.
