Stone Guide

Picking the perfect counter stone

Home / Stone Guide

When you are choosing a countertop, the stone you select does more than add beauty to your kitchen or bath. It influences how the room feels, how durable the surface is, how easy it is to maintain, and how well it fits your everyday routine. At Tommy Construction, we help homeowners compare the most popular stone options so they can make a confident choice for their space.

We carry premium granite, marble, and quartz, each offering a different mix of performance, character, and long-term value. The right fit depends on how you use the space, the level of maintenance you want, and the look you want to create.

Granite

Granite is a natural stone known for its toughness, heat resistance, and rich variation. Each slab has unique speckling and patterns, making it ideal for homeowners who want a durable surface with character.

Marble

Marble adds a more luxurious look with elegant veining and a soft, luminous finish. It is a classic option for those who want a statement surface in a kitchen, bath, or elegant entertaining space.

Quartz

Quartz is an engineered stone made to be more consistent, durable, and low-maintenance. It is ideal for busy households that want a polished look without the same level of care as natural stone.

Choosing the perfect stone for your home

The best stone for your project depends on how the space is used and the mood you want to create. A busy family kitchen may benefit from quartz because it is durable and easy to clean. A homeowner wanting a more classic, high-end statement may prefer marble for its distinctive veining and elegance. Granite remains a popular option for those who want natural variation and a highly resilient surface.

Consider your lifestyle

If you cook often, host gatherings, or want a surface that can handle spills and daily wear, durability and upkeep are key. Quartz is often chosen for easy maintenance, while granite remains a favorite for its toughness and natural beauty.

Think about the look you want

Color, pattern, and finish all shape how your room feels. Marble creates a refined, luxurious look, granite offers more natural movement and texture, and quartz gives you a wide range of consistent design options.

Plan around maintenance

Natural stone is beautiful, but it does require care. Marble is softer and more porous than granite or quartz, so it may need extra attention. Quartz and granite are often chosen for easier daily upkeep.

Match the stone to the room

A kitchen island, a vanity top, or a fireplace surround all benefit from different looks and performance traits. We can help you match the right material to each project and design style.

Our recommendation

Start with how you live in the space

If you want a surface that balances beauty, durability, and low maintenance, quartz is a strong choice. If you want a natural stone look with unique patterning and a timeless feel, granite or marble may be the best fit. We can walk you through the options and help you find the one that matches both your style and your routine.
