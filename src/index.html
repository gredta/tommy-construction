Stone Surfaces

Granite, Marble & Quartz

Tommy Construction fabricates and installs high-quality stone surfaces for kitchens, baths, and custom interior features, with the finish and durability to match your space.

  • Granite

    A hard, heat-resistant natural stone that delivers lasting durability, rich color variation, and a premium look for countertops, vanities, and fireplace surrounds.

  • Marble

    Known for its elegant veining and classic luxury, marble is ideal for statement surfaces when you want a refined, timeless finish in kitchens and baths.

  • Quartz

    A durable, low-maintenance engineered stone available in a wide range of colors and finishes, perfect for busy kitchens and high-use spaces.
